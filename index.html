🌱 Start Here

Welcome. Islam is a way of life based on worshipping Allah alone, following His guidance and following the example of His Messenger Muhammad ﷺ.

1. What is Islam?

Islam means submitting yourself to Allah, worshipping Him alone, and following His guidance.

2. Who is Allah?

Allah is the one true God and the Creator of everything. Muslims worship Allah alone.

English: Allah is the One worthy of worship.
اردو: اللہ ہی وہ واحد ذات ہے جو عبادت کے لائق ہے۔

3. The Shahadah

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ
Transliteration:
Ashhadu an la ilaha illallah, wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan rasulullah.
English:
I testify that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah, and I testify that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.
اردو:
میں گواہی دیتا ہوں کہ اللہ کے سوا کوئی معبودِ برحق نہیں، اور میں گواہی دیتا ہوں کہ محمد ﷺ اللہ کے رسول ہیں۔

4. The Five Pillars of Islam

1. Shahadah
Testimony of faith.
2. Salah
Performing the five daily prayers.
3. Zakat
Giving obligatory charity when eligible.
4. Sawm
Fasting during Ramadan.
5. Hajj
Pilgrimage to Makkah for those who are able.

🕌 Learn Salah

Salah is the daily prayer performed by Muslims. A new Muslim can learn it gradually, step by step.

Before Salah

1. Make Wudu
Perform ablution when required.
2. Face the Qiblah
Face toward the Ka'bah in Makkah.
3. Make the intention
Know in your heart which prayer you are performing.

Starting the Prayer

الله أكبر
Allahu Akbar
English: Allah is the Greatest.
اردو: اللہ سب سے بڑا ہے۔

Surah Al-Fatihah

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ
الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ
إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ
اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ
صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ
غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ
English:
All praise is for Allah, Lord of the worlds. The Most Merciful, the Especially Merciful. Master of the Day of Judgment. You alone we worship and You alone we ask for help. Guide us to the straight path.
اردو:
سب تعریفیں اللہ کے لیے ہیں جو تمام جہانوں کا رب ہے۔ نہایت مہربان، بہت رحم فرمانے والا۔ روزِ جزا کا مالک۔ ہم صرف تیری عبادت کرتے ہیں اور صرف تجھ ہی سے مدد مانگتے ہیں۔ ہمیں سیدھا راستہ دکھا۔

Ruku

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ
Glory is to my Lord, the Most Great.

Sujood

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى
Glory is to my Lord, the Most High.

Ending the Prayer

السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ
May the peace and mercy of Allah be upon you.

Note: This is an introductory learning page. Detailed prayer instructions and differences in recognized schools of Islamic jurisprudence should be reviewed carefully before final publication.

💧 Learn Wudu

Wudu is the ritual purification performed before certain acts of worship, including Salah.

Step 1 — Intention
Make the intention for purification in your heart.
Step 2 — Wash the hands
Wash both hands thoroughly.
Step 3 — Rinse the mouth
Rinse the mouth.
Step 4 — Clean the nose
Take water into the nose and clean it.
Step 5 — Wash the face
Wash the entire face.
Step 6 — Wash the arms
Wash the arms including the elbows.
Step 7 — Wipe the head
Wipe the head.
Step 8 — Wipe the ears
Wipe the ears.
Step 9 — Wash the feet
Wash both feet including the ankles.
Important: The exact details and recommended sequence can vary in some matters between recognized schools of Islamic jurisprudence.

🤲 Daily Duas

Before Eating

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

Transliteration: Bismillah

English: In the name of Allah.

اردو: اللہ کے نام سے۔

Before Sleeping

بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا

Transliteration: Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya.

English: In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live.

اردو: اے اللہ! تیرے ہی نام کے ساتھ مرتا اور جیتا ہوں۔

Seeking Forgiveness

أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ

Transliteration: Astaghfirullah.

English: I seek forgiveness from Allah.

اردو: میں اللہ سے بخشش مانگتا ہوں۔
