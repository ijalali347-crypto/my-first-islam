LESSON 1

🌱 Start Here

Welcome to your journey of learning Islam. You do not need to learn everything in one day. Start with the foundations and continue step by step.

What is Islam?

Islam is submission to Allah and obedience to His guidance. Muslims worship Allah alone and follow the guidance brought by His Messenger Muhammad ﷺ.

Who is Allah?

Allah is the One true God, the Creator and Sustainer of everything. He alone deserves worship.

English:
Allah is the One worthy of worship.
اردو:
اللہ ہی وہ واحد ذات ہے جو عبادت کے لائق ہے۔

The Shahadah

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ
Transliteration:
Ashhadu an la ilaha illallah, wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan rasulullah.
English:
I testify that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah, and I testify that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.
اردو:
میں گواہی دیتا ہوں کہ اللہ کے سوا کوئی معبودِ برحق نہیں، اور میں گواہی دیتا ہوں کہ محمد ﷺ اللہ کے رسول ہیں۔

The Five Pillars of Islam

1
Shahadah
The testimony of faith.
2
Salah
The five daily prayers.
3
Zakat
Obligatory charity for those who meet its conditions.
4
Sawm
Fasting during Ramadan.
5
Hajj
Pilgrimage to Makkah for those who are able.

The Six Articles of Faith

Muslims believe in:

1. Allah
2. His angels
3. His books
4. His messengers
5. The Last Day
6. Divine decree (Al-Qadar), its good and its difficult aspects.
LESSON 2

🕌 Learn Salah

Salah is one of the Five Pillars of Islam. Muslims perform five obligatory daily prayers.

Before Salah

1
Purification
Perform Wudu when it is required.
2
Clothing
Wear clothing that fulfills the requirements for prayer.
3
Place
Pray in a clean place.
4
Qiblah
Face toward the Ka'bah in Makkah.
5
Intention
Have the intention in your heart for the prayer you are performing.

Takbeer

اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
Allahu Akbar
English: Allah is the Greatest.
اردو: اللہ سب سے بڑا ہے۔

Ruku

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ
Subhana Rabbiyal-'Azim
English: Glory is to my Lord, the Most Great.
اردو: پاک ہے میرا رب، عظمت والا۔

Sujood

سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى
Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la
English: Glory is to my Lord, the Most High.
اردو: پاک ہے میرا رب، سب سے بلند۔

Salam — Ending the Prayer

السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ
Assalamu 'alaykum wa rahmatullah
English: Peace and the mercy of Allah be upon you.
اردو: تم پر سلامتی اور اللہ کی رحمت ہو۔
Beginner tip: Learn the prayer gradually. Start by learning the movements and the words, then practice until you become comfortable.
LESSON 3

💧 Learn Wudu

Wudu is the ritual purification performed before prayer when purification is required.

1
Intention
Make the intention for purification in your heart.
2
Wash the hands
Wash both hands thoroughly.
3
Rinse the mouth
Rinse the mouth with water.
4
Clean the nose
Clean the nose with water.
5
Wash the face
Wash the whole face.
6
Wash the arms
Wash the arms including the elbows.
7
Wipe the head
Wipe the head with wet hands.
8
Wipe the ears
Wipe the ears.
9
Wash the feet
Wash both feet including the ankles.
Important: Some details of Wudu have differences among recognized schools of Islamic jurisprudence. The app will later include more detailed explanations for beginners.
LESSON 4

🤲 Daily Duas

Start with a few simple supplications and learn their meanings.

Before Eating

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ
Bismillah
English: In the name of Allah.
اردو: اللہ کے نام سے۔

Before Sleeping

اللَّهُمَّ بِاسْمِكَ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا
Allahumma bismika amutu wa ahya
English: O Allah, in Your name I die and I live.
اردو: اے اللہ! تیرے ہی نام کے ساتھ مرتا اور جیتا ہوں۔

Seeking Forgiveness

أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ
Astaghfirullah
English: I seek forgiveness from Allah.
اردو: میں اللہ سے بخشش مانگتا ہوں۔

When Leaving Home

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ، لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ
Bismillah, tawakkaltu 'alallah, la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah
English: In the name of Allah. I place my trust in Allah. There is no power and no strength except with Allah.
اردو: اللہ کے نام سے۔ میں نے اللہ پر بھروسہ کیا۔ اللہ کے سوا نہ کوئی طاقت ہے نہ قوت۔
